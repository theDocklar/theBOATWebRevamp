## Frequently Asked Questions

**Shopify vs WooCommerce for Sri Lankan stores?**  
Shopify is hosted (99.99% uptime, automatic updates, PCI compliance included). WooCommerce is self-hosted (you manage security, backups, scaling). For most businesses, Shopify's reliability and lower operational overhead win. WooCommerce makes sense if you need extreme customization or already have WordPress infrastructure.

**Can you migrate our existing store to Shopify?**  
Yes. We've migrated stores from WooCommerce, Magento, custom PHP, and even spreadsheet-based systems. Migration includes products, customers, order history, and SEO redirects. Timeline: 2-4 weeks depending on store size.

**What about payment gateways for Sri Lankan customers?**  
Shopify Payments works with Stripe (supports Sri Lankan rupees). We also integrate local gateways (PayHere, Genie, bank gateways) and cash-on-delivery flows. Multi-currency support for international customers.

**Do we need Shopify Plus for a large catalog?**  
Not necessarily. Shopify Standard handles thousands of products fine. Plus is for businesses needing custom checkout experiences, wholesale portals, or multi-storefront management. We'll advise based on your requirements—we don't upsell Plus unless it's genuinely needed.

**What's your post-launch support like?**  
30 days included for bug fixes and minor adjustments. After that, optional retainers ($500–$1,500/month) cover ongoing updates, A/B testing, seasonal campaign setup, and performance monitoring. Most clients stay on retainer because e-commerce is never "done."
